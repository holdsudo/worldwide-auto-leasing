SMS terms & conditions

The text messaging program run by Worldwide Auto Leasing.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Program name and sender

Worldwide Auto Leasing Alerts, operated by Worldwide Auto Leasing ("we", "us"). Questions: (732) 534-4430, info@worldwideautoleasing.com, Mon–Fri 9am–5pm Eastern.

Who can join

The program is for people 18 or older with a U.S. mobile number who have asked us about leasing a vehicle. Joining is optional.

How you opt in

You opt in by checking the unchecked, optional SMS consent box on a form at worldwideautoleasing.com (the quote forms and the specials request form), or by giving a team member your express consent, which we confirm by text. Consent is not a condition of any purchase, lease or quote. You can use every form on this site without agreeing to texts.

By opting in you consent to receive text messages, which may be sent using an automated system, at the number you provided. We keep a record of the consent text shown, the date and time, the page and the device used.

Types of messages

  • Replies to your quote or specials request and follow-up on your inquiry
  • Lease specials and program information for brands you asked about
  • Vehicle availability updates
  • Appointment, document, signing and delivery reminders
  • Customer care and support messages

We do not send messages about unrelated products, and we do not send messages to numbers that have not opted in.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies with your activity. During an active quote or delivery you may receive several messages a week. Otherwise messages are occasional.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge for the program. Check your mobile plan for details.

How to opt out

Reply STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT to any message. You will receive one confirmation text and no further messages. To rejoin, reply START or opt in again on our site. You can also opt out by emailing info@worldwideautoleasing.com or calling (732) 534-4430.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, email info@worldwideautoleasing.com or call (732) 534-4430, Mon–Fri 9am–5pm Eastern.

Carriers

Supported carriers include AT&T, T-Mobile, Verizon and most regional U.S. carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We use your mobile number and opt-in data only to run this program and to serve your request. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party. See our Privacy Policy.

Changes

We may update these terms. The current version always lives at this address, and the date above shows when it last changed. See also our Terms of Service.
